    Price average

    The two prices shown on each card. Each binder has its own choice. Raw conditions come from TCGplayer sales, graded ones from eBay sold listings via PriceCharting.

    PSA slab check

    Each price is the average of the newest matching Unlimited English sales (up to 5 per condition). 1st Edition, Shadowless, Japanese and lots are skipped.

    The Refresh prices button at the top checks only the binder you have open.

    Backup

    Download backup
    One file with everything, for every binder. Keep a copy somewhere other than the server. Download spreadsheet gives you the binders you pick as an Excel file (a tab for each binder) to open anywhere. Download for TCGplayer gives you a list of the cards you own, for Collection → Import in the TCGplayer app.

    Saved on the server

    Made automatically before each update, import, restore and phone sync, and before a binder starts fresh. To save space, the ones before updates, imports and syncs leave out your photos (those never change your photos), and restoring one of those keeps the photos you have. One daily backup a week includes them.
