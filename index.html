    Privacy
    Blurs cards owned, % complete, collection value and cost to finish, shows every card in color, and hides Owned/Need tags, the shopping list, stats, and your purchases. Only affects this device. The eye button at the top does the same thing.
    Cards
    Card images come from the public card database, which uses 1st Edition scans for older sets. The label is a reminder that you collect Unlimited.
    Tab icon
    The little picture on the browser tab. A square PNG works best. It's saved on BEN only.
    Price average
    Pick the two prices shown on each card. Each binder keeps its own choice, so switch sets from the menu to change another one. Raw conditions come from TCGplayer sales. Graded prices come from eBay sold listings collected by PriceCharting, sorted by the grading company and grade in each listing's title.
    Each refresh also gets TCGplayer's market price and Cardmarket's 30-day average from TCGdex, a free card database. They show on each card as a second opinion. When a card has no recent sales for your price options, this uses TCGplayer's market price instead of keeping an old price. Applies to every binder.
